**Credit Card Financial Dashboard**
This project features a comprehensive and interactive dashboard built to analyze credit card transactions and customer demographics. Using raw transactional and customer data, the dashboard provides actionable business insights into revenue generation, user spending behaviors, and demographic trends.

**Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)**
**Total Revenue:** The total revenue generated stands at 55M.  Total Interest Earned: The business earned 7.84M in total interest.  
**Total Customer Income:** The combined income of the customer base is 576M.  Transaction Volume: A total transaction amount of 45M was recorded across 656K individual transactions.  

**Customer InsightsTop** 
**Occupations:** Businessmen and White-collar professionals are the highest revenue-contributing customer groups.  
**Income Groups:** Customers categorized within the "High" income bracket are the primary drivers of revenue.  
**Geographic Distribution:** Texas (TX), New York (NY), and California (CA) represent the top three states for revenue generation.  
**Marital Status:** Married customers generate a higher portion of revenue (15M) compared to single customers (12M).  

**Transaction TrendsCard** 
**Popularity:** The "Blue" card category is the most widely used, driving over 46M in revenue alone.  
**Top Expenses:** The majority of credit card spending is directed toward Bills, Entertainment, and Fuel.  
**Usage Methods:** Swipe transactions account for the bulk of revenue (35M), vastly outperforming Chip (17M) and Online payments.  
**Quarterly Consistency:** Revenue distribution remains highly stable across the year, maintaining around 2.5K per quarter from Q1 to Q4.
